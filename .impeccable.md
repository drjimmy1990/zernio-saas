# Design context

Confirmed by the product owner on 19 October 2026.

- Audience: business owners. They need a dashboard that is simple, professional and easy to learn.
- Core jobs: connect business accounts, publish posts, reply to customers and manage campaigns.
- Product: Asra3, a SaaS using Zernio provider integrations, deployed at asra3.com.
- Interface direction: clear task hierarchy, familiar labels, restrained professional presentation and progressive disclosure of advanced options.
- Compatibility: Arabic and English, right-to-left layout, existing light/dark preferences, desktop and mobile.
- State: show actual provider progress, failures and recovery actions. A visual success state must correspond to confirmed evidence.

Refine each phase's dashboard alongside its real integration. Preserve existing customer work, accessibility and the ability to recover from interrupted requests.
